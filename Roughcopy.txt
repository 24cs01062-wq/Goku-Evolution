        <img src="oozaru.jpg" alt="image of oozaru form goku" width="400">
        <p><em>The Great Ape transformation allows Saiyans with tails to transform into enormous ape-like creatures when exposed to</br> sufficient Blutz Waves, traditionally through a full moon. This transformation dramatically increases their physical strength</br> but can make them difficult to control.</em></p>

        <h2>3.Super Saiyan</h2>
        <img src="supersaiyan1.jpg" alt="image of supersaiyan Goku" width="400">
        <p><em>One of the most legendary transformations in anime history. Goku first achieved Super Saiyan on Planet Namek after<br> witnessing Frieza kill Krillin. His hair turned golden, his eyes became turquoise, and his power increased tremendously.<br> This transformation marked a turning point in Dragon Ball Z.</em></p>

        <h2>4.Super Saiyan 2:</h2>
        <img src="supersaiyan2.jpg" alt="image of base form goku" width="400">
        <p><em>Super Saiyan 2 is an advanced evolution of Super Saiyan. It features sharper golden hair and visible electrical sparks<br> surrounding the user. Goku achieved this form during his training in the Other World, and it provides a significant increase in power.</em></p>

        <h2>5.Super Saiyan 3</h2>
        <img src="supersaiyan3.jpg" alt="image of oozaru form goku" width="400">
        <p><em>Super Saiyan 3 is one of Goku's most visually distinctive transformations. It gives him extremely long golden hair and<br> removes his eyebrows. The form provides enormous power but consumes energy rapidly, making it difficult to maintain.</em></p>

        <h2>6.Super Saiyan god</h2>
        <img src="supersaiyanred.jpg" alt="image of supersaiyan Goku" width="400">
        <p><em>Super Saiyan God introduces Goku to divine energy known as God Ki. This transformation gives him red hair, a slimmer<br> appearance and a calm aura. Goku first achieved it through a ritual involving five righteous Saiyans sharing their energy with him.</em></p>

        <h2>7.Super Saiyan blue</h2>
        <img src="supersaiyanblue.jpg" alt="image of base form goku" width="400">
        <p><em>Also known as Super Saiyan God Super Saiyan, this transformation combines the power of Super Saiyan God with the<br> Super Saiyan transformation. It gives Goku blue hair and allows him to use divine energy while transformed.</em></p>

        <h2>8.Ultra Instinct</h2>
        <img src="ultrainstinct.jpg" alt="image of oozaru form goku" width="400">
        <p><em>Ultra Instinct is an extraordinary technique that allows Goku's body to react independently of conscious thought. Instead<br> of deliberately deciding how to dodge or attack, his body responds automatically. Its mastered form is recognizable by Goku's silver hair and eyes.</em></p>

        <h2>Conclusion:</h2>
